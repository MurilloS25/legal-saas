# Product Rules

## Product Position

The application supports legal productivity for independent lawyers in Costa Rica. It does not replace professional legal judgment.

## Allowed Product Data

- Structured client metadata needed for reuse.
- Structured template metadata.
- Structured notarial record metadata needed for index preparation.
- Structured receivable metadata.
- Account and user settings.

## Disallowed Product Data

- Stored generated legal documents.
- Full sensitive escritura content.
- Digital signatures.
- Official legal submission payloads.
- Official notarial index submission payloads.

## Product Non-Goals

- No official government or notarial submissions.
- No assumption of legal responsibility.
- No AI legal advice.
- No self-hosted production Docker deployment.
